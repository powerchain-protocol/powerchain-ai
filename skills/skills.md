# PowerChain AI Skills Registry

This registry documents all operational skills and capabilities integrated into the PowerChain AI Operating System.

## Integrated Capabilities

1. **Grid Telemetry & Load Balancing** (`grid-telemetry`)
   - Autonomous frequency regulation, microgrid load balancing, and battery dispatch cycle management.

2. **Solana Actions & Blinks** (`solana-actions`)
   - On-chain energy credit minting and direct peer-to-peer settlement via Solana Actions.

3. **Pyth Network Oracle Integration** (`pyth-oracles`)
   - High-precision price feeds for SOL, PWRC Token, and kWh energy credits.

4. **Google Workspace Gmail Integration** (`gmail-notifications`)
   - Automated settlement receipts, PDF report dispatch, and critical grid telemetry alerts.

5. **Multi-MPC Vault Security** (`mpc-security`)
   - Threshold signatures for treasury transactions and automated fee routing.
